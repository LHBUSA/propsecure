ENTERPRISE FAQ

What risk, product, and technical teams need to know.

PropSecure is designed as managed operating infrastructure. The identity foundation is broad; each surveillance program is explicit about sources, coverage, evidence, rules, delivery, and responsibility.

What is PropSecure?

PropSecure is a managed property change intelligence layer. It establishes trusted identity, monitors configured signals, applies customer-owned decision rules, and routes evidence-backed exceptions into the systems and human-review paths your enterprise already governs.

Is PropSecure a self-serve data feed?

No. APIs, webhooks, queues, and secure files are delivery mechanisms inside a scoped surveillance program. The deployment includes source mapping, jurisdiction coverage, cadence, rules, evidence standards, routing, and operating requirements.

Does every signal have global coverage?

No vague global claim is made. PropData provides a 225M+ identity foundation across the United States, Great Britain, New Zealand, and Australia. PropSecure surveillance is engineered and documented by jurisdiction, source availability, and refresh cadence.

How does a pilot work?

We define the portfolio, jurisdictions, priority signals, evidence requirements, decision rules, and delivery paths. The program can begin in governed shadow mode so your team can measure event quality and workflow impact before production decisions depend on it.

Does PropSecure make automated adverse decisions?

No. PropSecure provides verified identity, evidence-backed events, configurable decision support, and routing. Your organization retains its policy, governance, human-review requirements, and final underwriting, servicing, title, or portfolio decisions.

How are events delivered?

Qualified events can be delivered through versioned APIs, webhooks, queues, secure files, case systems, CRMs, underwriting desks, servicing platforms, Slack, or another agreed destination.
