What it does

One security layer for every property you touch.

PropSecure is being designed to turn fragmented property signals into verified identity, continuous monitoring, and clear action for enterprise teams.

Property Verification

Confirm the property, parcel, owner, and record identity before your workflow moves forward.

  • Parcel-level identity matching
  • Ownership and address validation
  • Auditable verification signals

Portfolio Monitoring

Watch assets continuously and know when property identity or public-record activity changes.
