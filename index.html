What is PropSecure?

PropSecure is a managed enterprise property surveillance integration from PropTechUSA.ai. It establishes a trusted parcel identity, monitors configured risk signals, applies your decision rules, and routes evidence-backed exceptions into your existing workflows.

How does PropSecure verify a property?

PropSecure is designed to resolve address and parcel identifiers against PropData’s nationwide property intelligence network, then establish a trusted parcel-level record for downstream workflows.

What property changes can PropSecure monitor?

PropSecure can be configured around ownership and deed activity, involuntary liens and defaults, tax assessment and delinquency, valuation and equity thresholds, natural hazards, permits, code violations, and other scoped property-risk signals. Exact coverage and refresh timing are established by jurisdiction during integration design.

Who is PropSecure built for?

PropSecure is built for PropTech platforms, appraisal and valuation teams, investors and portfolio operators, lenders and servicers, insurers and risk teams, and enterprises with property-critical workflows.

Does PropSecure support nationwide coverage?

PropData’s 166M+ property record network provides a nationwide identity foundation. Individual surveillance signals have different jurisdiction-level availability and refresh cadences, so every enterprise deployment receives a documented coverage scope instead of a vague nationwide claim.

Is PropSecure just an API?

No. APIs and webhooks are delivery mechanisms inside a broader implementation. PropSecure scopes your portfolio and jurisdictions, configures signal coverage and decision rules, establishes evidence and escalation requirements, integrates with your systems, and supports the surveillance program after deployment.

How is a PropSecure enterprise integration deployed?

We begin with technical discovery covering portfolio scope, jurisdictions, risk policy, signal priorities, system destinations, and service requirements. We then implement the monitoring and routing architecture in phases with documented coverage, acceptance criteria, and operational rules.
