What is PropSecure?

PropSecure is a B2B property security platform from PropTechUSA.ai that helps teams verify property identity, monitor portfolio changes, and route property risk alerts into enterprise workflows.

How does PropSecure verify a property?

PropSecure is designed to resolve address and parcel identifiers against PropData’s nationwide property intelligence network, then establish a trusted parcel-level record for downstream workflows.

What property changes can PropSecure monitor?

PropSecure is designed to monitor ownership changes, recorded-document activity, parcel identity, and configurable risk signals. Exact signal availability and refresh timing may vary by jurisdiction and integration configuration.

Who is PropSecure built for?

PropSecure is built for PropTech platforms, appraisal and valuation teams, investors and portfolio operators, lenders and servicers, and enterprise teams with property-critical workflows.

Does PropSecure support nationwide coverage?

PropSecure is designed for nationwide United States coverage using PropData’s 166M+ property record network. Data depth and refresh timing may vary by jurisdiction.

Does PropSecure support API integrations?

Yes. PropSecure is API-first infrastructure for integrating property verification, monitoring, and risk events into existing products and enterprise workflows.
